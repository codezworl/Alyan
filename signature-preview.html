⚙️ Gmail Setup Instructions

  1. Click the Copy Signature button above.
  2. In your browser, go to mail.google.com.
  3. Click the Gear icon → See all settings.
  4. Under the General tab, scroll down to Signature.
  5. Click + Create new, name it, and paste (Ctrl+V) into the box.
  6. Set Signature defaults (For new emails & On reply/forward).
  7. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes.
